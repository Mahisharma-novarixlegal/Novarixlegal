How to file an FIR — and what to do if police refuse

The First Information Report is the entry point for every criminal matter. Most people do not know their rights when they walk into a police station.

CriminalPoliceBNSS 2023
Read the procedure
  1. Go to the police station in whose jurisdiction the offence occurred.
  2. State your complaint orally or in writing — the SHO is bound to record it.
  3. Under Section 173 BNSS, if police refuse, send your complaint by post to the Superintendent of Police.
  4. If still refused, file a complaint before the Magistrate under Section 175 BNSS.
  5. Once registered, you are entitled to a free copy of the FIR immediately.
  6. Retain the FIR number — it is your reference for all future proceedings.

How to apply for anticipatory bail

Section 482 BNSS (formerly S.438 CrPC) — one of the most powerful protections in criminal law. Here is exactly how to use it.

CriminalBailSessions / HC
Read the procedure
  1. File an application before the Sessions Court or High Court anticipating arrest.
  2. State the apprehension of arrest and reasons the accusation is false or exaggerated.
  3. Attach relevant documents — FIR copy, previous bail orders, personal affidavit.
  4. Court may issue an interim order directing that if arrested, the applicant shall be released on bail.
  5. Notice is issued to the prosecution for their response.
  6. Final hearing — court considers nature of accusation, antecedents and flight risk before deciding.

How to file a writ petition in the Delhi High Court

Writs under Article 226 are the most direct way to challenge government action or inaction. This is how it works before the Delhi High Court.

ConstitutionalWritDelhi HC
Read the procedure
  1. Identify the nature of writ: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Certiorari, Prohibition, or Quo Warranto.
  2. Draft the writ petition with a synopsis, list of dates and grounds. Attach all documents relied upon.
  3. File at the filing counter of the Delhi High Court. A W.P. number is assigned.
  4. On first hearing, the court may admit the matter and issue notice to the respondent.
  5. Counter affidavit is filed by the respondent. Rejoinder by petitioner if needed.
  6. Final arguments on maintainability and merits, followed by judgment.

How to file for divorce — all grounds and procedures

Divorce under the Hindu Marriage Act, Special Marriage Act or personal laws — the procedure differs significantly. Here is the full map.

Family LawMatrimonialHMA
Read the procedure
  1. Identify grounds: Mutual consent (S.13B HMA) or contested (cruelty, desertion, adultery under S.13).
  2. For mutual consent: both parties file a joint petition. 6-month cooling-off period applies (may be waived).
  3. For contested: file the petition in the Family Court where parties last resided together.
  4. Serve notice on the respondent — they file a written statement in reply.
  5. Mediation is typically attempted before trial begins.
  6. If mediation fails — evidence, cross-examination, arguments, then decree.

How to file a consumer complaint

The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 gives every buyer strong rights. Complaints can now be filed entirely online.

ConsumerNCDRCOnline Filing
Read the procedure
  1. Send a legal notice to the company first — often resolves without further proceedings.
  2. If unresolved, file at edaakhil.nic.in — India's online consumer complaint portal.
  3. Jurisdiction by claim value: District (up to ₹50L), State (₹50L–₹2Cr), National (above ₹2Cr).
  4. Upload complaint, ID proof, purchase documents and copy of notice sent.
  5. Pay nominal filing fee. Notice is issued to the opposite party.
  6. Arguments and evidence, followed by order — compensation, refund, or replacement.

How to file an SLP in the Supreme Court of India

A Special Leave Petition under Article 136 — the route to the Supreme Court when the High Court has ruled against you.

Supreme CourtSLPArticle 136
Read the procedure
  1. Obtain a certified copy of the impugned High Court judgment immediately.
  2. File within 90 days (civil) or 60 days (criminal) of the HC judgment at the SC Registry.
  3. Petition includes: synopsis, list of dates, grounds of challenge and prayer clause.
  4. Listed before a two-judge Bench — they decide whether to grant leave to appeal.
  5. If leave is granted: converted to Civil/Criminal Appeal and heard on full merits.
  6. If refused: HC order stands. Review petition possible if error is apparent on record.
